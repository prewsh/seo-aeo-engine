# Example: SEO/AEO Landing Page Writer

The canonical implementation lives at `.agent/skills/landing-page-writer/SKILL.md` and is mirrored by `seo-aeo-landing-page.md` for older consumers.

The writer produces, in order:

1. SEO metadata and URL slug
2. Hero with a factual direct-answer block
3. Problem, solution, features, and user outcomes
4. Trust evidence and CTAs
5. How-it-works steps
6. Fair comparison or alternatives
7. Direct FAQ answers
8. Internal-link suggestions

It must use supplied facts only. Missing proof, pricing, customer evidence, or product capabilities must be labelled as placeholders or verification notes rather than invented. The primary keyword is used naturally in important page elements without an arbitrary density target.
