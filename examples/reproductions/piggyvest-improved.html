FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PiggyVest?

PiggyVest is a Nigerian online savings and investment app used by over 6 million people. It lets you automate savings, lock funds for higher interest, set savings goals, and save in both naira and dollars — all from your phone.

How does saving work on PiggyVest?

You fund your PiggyVest wallet and choose a savings product. Piggybank deducts automatically on a schedule, Safelock locks your money for a fixed period, and Target lets you save toward a named goal. You earn interest on all savings.

Can I withdraw my money at any time?

It depends on the product. Flex Naira and Flex Dollar allow withdrawals anytime. Piggybank has a quarterly withdrawal window. Safelock is locked until your chosen maturity date. Each product clearly shows its terms before you commit.

Is PiggyVest safe and regulated?

Yes. PiggyVest is regulated by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) and uses 256-bit SSL encryption — the same standard used by major banks. It has processed over ₦1 trillion in user funds since launch.

Does PiggyVest charge fees?

Creating an account and saving are free. A small fee applies on early withdrawals from certain products. There are no monthly maintenance fees and no minimum balance requirement to open an account.

Who is PiggyVest for?

PiggyVest is for any Nigerian who earns money and wants to keep more of it — salaried employees, freelancers, students, and business owners. If you find it hard to save consistently, PiggyVest is designed specifically for you.
